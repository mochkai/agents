## Goal
Support exponentiation in the calculator.

## Requirements
- Add `power(base, exp)` to calc.js and export it. `exp` must be a non-negative integer, otherwise throw Error("Invalid exponent").

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] power(2, 10) === 1024, power(5, 0) === 1
- [ ] power(2, -1) and power(2, 1.5) throw "Invalid exponent"
- [ ] `npm test` passes

## Out of scope
- Changes to other functions.
